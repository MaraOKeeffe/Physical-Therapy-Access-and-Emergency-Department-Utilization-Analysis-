This analysis identifies associations between physical therapist availability, rurality, geographic distribution, and emergency department utilization. It establishes correlation rather than causation. CMS data include only providers enrolled with Medicare and therefore do not represent the entire physical therapy workforce. In addition, the project combines data from multiple years because comparable datasets were not available for every source (for example, 2024 CMS provider data, 2022 AHRQ emergency department data, 2020 Census population estimates, and 2020 RUCA classifications). Other factors that may influence emergency department utilization, including insurance coverage; socioeconomic status; age distribution; availability of other healthcare providers; and regional healthcare infrastructure, were outside the scope of this analysis.
